# Aesthetic Profile: Playful & Child-Friendly

This profile defines a vibrant, welcoming, and highly accessible visual aesthetic designed for children or educational tools.

## Core Characteristics
- **Shapes**: Heavily rounded corners (pill shapes) for all buttons, cards, and containers. Avoid sharp edges.
- **Color Palette**: Bright, cheerful primary and secondary colors (sunny yellows, bright reds, sky blues, grassy greens). High saturation is encouraged.
- **Typography**: Large, friendly, and highly legible fonts (e.g., rounded sans-serifs like Quicksand or Nunito). Increased font sizes globally.
- **Visuals**: Use of playful icons, illustrations, and clear visual metaphors over dense text.
- **Interactions**: Bouncy, delightful animations. Large touch targets for interactive elements to accommodate developing motor skills. 

## Application
When a project specifies this aesthetic, prioritize fun, engagement, and extreme ease of use. The interface should feel like a toy: intuitive, safe, and colorful.
