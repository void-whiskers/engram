# Galilean Gravity

## Core Concept
In a uniform gravitational field, all objects experience the same constant acceleration regardless of their mass, provided that other forces (like air resistance) are absent.

## Key Principles
- **Mass Independence**: A heavier object does not fall faster than a lighter object in a vacuum. The gravitational force scales linearly with mass, but inertia also scales linearly, canceling out the effect on acceleration.
- **Equivalence Principle**: This observation laid the groundwork for Einstein's equivalence principle in General Relativity.

## Foundational Experiments
- **Apollo 15 Hammer and Feather Drop (1971)**: See [EXP-1971-001](../../experiments/1971/exp-1971-001-apollo-15-hammer-feather.md). Commander David Scott demonstrated this principle visually on the Moon, proving the theory holds perfectly in a vacuum.
