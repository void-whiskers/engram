# Wave Mechanics Theory

## Core Concept
Light and other forms of electromagnetic radiation propagate as waves, exhibiting behaviors such as interference, diffraction, and polarization.

## Key Principles
- **Interference**: When two waves meet, they superimpose to form a new wave. This can be constructive (peaks align, increasing amplitude) or destructive (peak meets trough, decreasing amplitude).
- **Diffraction**: The bending of waves around obstacles or through openings.

## Foundational Experiments
- **Thomas Young's Double-Slit Experiment (1801)**: See [EXP-1801-001](../../experiments/1801/exp-1801-001-double-slit-interference.md). This provided the definitive proof that light behaves as a wave, superseding the purely corpuscular (particle) theory.
