# FUTURE-004: String Theory Multiverse Probe

## Motivation
Current unifying physical theories point toward M-theory, which posits 11 dimensions and potentially a "landscape" of multiverses. We need empirical evidence to bridge the gap between elegant mathematical frameworks and observable physical reality.

## Hypothesis
If our universe is a D-brane within a higher-dimensional bulk, high-energy collisions or specific gravitational wave signatures from the early universe (Cosmic Microwave Background) will reveal "leakage" of gravitons into extra dimensions, or signatures of collisions with adjacent branes.

## Proposed Method
1. Construct a next-generation particle accelerator capable of energies orders of magnitude higher than the LHC (e.g., a circumsolar particle collider).
2. Develop hyper-sensitive gravitational wave interferometers situated in deep space (e.g., LISA successors) to map the stochastic gravitational wave background with extreme precision.
3. Search for missing energy signatures in collisions that would indicate graviton emission into the bulk.

## Success Criteria
Detection of statistically significant "missing energy" in closed-system high-energy collisions, or identification of non-Gaussianities in the CMB polarization corresponding to brane-collision models.

## Notes
- This is a multi-generational experiment requiring infrastructure not currently possible to build.
- Funding required: approximately the GDP of a medium-sized solar system.
